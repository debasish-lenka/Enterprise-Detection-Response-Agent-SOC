🧪 CyberChef — Analyst Decode / Transform Toolkit
Static tool · no backend, nothing leaves the browser
Open CyberChef ↗ ← Portal
CyberChef is a client-side "Swiss army knife" — it has no server, no logs, no stats to report, so this page is a quick-reference launcher rather than a KPI dashboard. Drag "recipes" together in the real tool to decode/decrypt/transform artifacts during an investigation.
